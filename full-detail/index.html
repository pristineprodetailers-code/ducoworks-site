What a full detail covers

Everything a wash does, and then the work a wash never touches.

A car wash removes what is loose. A detail removes what is bonded — the iron particles from brake dust that have embedded in your clear coat, the tar down the sills, the film on the inside of the windscreen you only notice driving into the sun.

  • Snow foam pre-wash and two-bucket hand wash — the loose grit comes off before anything touches the paint.
  • Chemical decontamination — iron fallout and tar dissolved rather than scrubbed.
  • Clay bar — the paint is left glass smooth. Run your hand over it afterwards; it is the part people do not expect.
  • Machine-applied sealant — around six months of protection, not six weeks.
  • Interior deep clean — vents, seals, console and boot, not just the bits you can see from the driver's seat.
  • Leather cleaned and conditioned, or fabric shampooed.
  • Wheels, arches and tyres dressed, glass cleaned in and out.

02

Who it is for

Three cars that always come out best.

  • The family car. School runs, beach trips, spilled everything. Sand in the seat rails and a boot nobody has looked in since Christmas.
  • The car about to be sold. The cheapest money you will ever spend on a sale. Buyers price a car on how it presents long before they price it on the logbook.
  • The car that has never had one. Two or three years of tropical sun and wet seasons, washed at home with a sponge. There is usually a much better car under there.

Five to six hours on site. If your car is already well kept and just needs to stay that way, the Maintenance Detail is the cheaper regular option.

04

Questions

How long does a full detail take?

Five to six hours for most cars. Larger or heavily neglected vehicles run longer, and that is priced in before the work starts rather than added afterwards.

Do you need power and water?

A standard power point is needed for machine polishing. Water is carried, so a tap is helpful but not essential.

What if it rains?

This is Far North Queensland, so wet days are planned around. Undercover space is ideal. If neither is available the booking is moved to the next dry day at no charge.

Can you get pet hair out?

Yes. It is an add-on rather than standard, because a car with a dog in it every day takes far longer than one without.

How often should I have one?

Once a year for most cars up here, with maintenance details in between. The tropics are harder on a car than most people allow for.
