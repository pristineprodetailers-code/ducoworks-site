    The Standard

    Precision

    Panels are taped before a machine touches them and the work is done in sections, not all at once. The result is checked under a light hard enough to show what daylight hides.

    Finish

    The surface is the product. A car leaves when the finish is right, not when the time slot ends. One car a day is the reason that is possible.

    Preserve

    Protection that outlasts collection day. Every coating comes with the wash routine that keeps it rated, because the maintenance is half the result.

    06

    Questions

    Do you come to me?

    Yes. DucoWorks is fully mobile across the Cassowary Coast, from Cairns in the north to Tully in the south. The work is done at your home or workplace.

    Do you need power and water?

    A standard power point is needed for machine polishing. Water is carried, so a tap is helpful but not essential.

    How long does a detail take?

    A maintenance detail runs two to three hours. A full detail is most of a day. Paint correction with a ceramic coating is one to two full days depending on the vehicle.

    What happens if it rains?

    This is Far North Queensland, so wet days are planned around. Undercover space is ideal. If neither is available the booking is moved to the next dry day at no charge.

    How long does a ceramic coating last?

    The standard coating is rated at three years, with a five-year option available. Both need an annual maintenance wash to hold their rating.

    How do I pay?

    Card, cash or bank transfer on completion. Correction and ceramic bookings take a deposit to hold the day.
